How RentHub.mv Works

RentHub.mv helps connect people who have items to rent with people looking to rent them. We provide a simple platform to advertise rental listings while owners and renters communicate directly.

How to Rent How to List

For Renters

Looking to Rent?

Finding rentals has never been easier. Browse hundreds of listings across different categories and contact owners directly.

1

Browse RentHub.mv or our social media pages

Explore live listings on our website, Instagram, or Facebook page.

2

Search by category, location, or item

Use filters to narrow down listings by island, category, or keyword.

3

View photos, price, description, and contact details

Each listing shows everything you need to know before reaching out.

4

Contact the owner directly

Reach out via WhatsApp or phone to ask questions or confirm availability.

5

Agree on rental terms and arrange pickup or delivery

Finalise the details directly with the owner - pricing, dates, and handover.

ⓘ

Please note: RentHub.mv does not process payments or bookings. All arrangements are made directly between the renter and the owner.
